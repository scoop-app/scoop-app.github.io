2.2. Scoop Kit (kit.scoop.app)

2.2.1. What Scoop Kit is

Scoop Kit lets creators with an Instagram professional account build a portfolio from their Instagram profile, posts and insights, and share it with brands at kit.scoop.app. Creators can also set up automations that reply to comments on their own posts and send links by direct message. Creators sign in with Instagram. There is no separate Scoop Kit password.

2.2.2. Information we collect when you sign in

  • Profile: username, name, account type, profile picture, bio, and follower, following and media counts
  • Content: your recent posts, Reels and active Stories, with their captions, media type, thumbnails, links and dates
  • Insights for your account: reach, profile views, accounts engaged, daily follower growth, and audience age, gender, country and city
  • Insights for your posts and Reels: reach, views, likes, comments, saves, shares and total interactions
  • An access token from Instagram, which we use to read the information above

We keep copies of your profile picture and of the post thumbnails shown in your portfolio, because the image links Instagram provides expire.

2.2.3. Information we collect if you turn on automations

Automations need two more Instagram permissions, which you grant separately by choosing "Connect messaging". After that, Instagram notifies us of new comments on your posts and new direct messages to your account.

  • When a comment or message belongs to one of your automations, we keep it, with the commenter's Instagram-scoped ID, so we can send the replies you set up and show you how the automation performed. This includes the time the link was opened and the browser's user agent. We do not collect IP addresses.
  • When a comment or message does not belong to any automation, we do not keep its content. We keep only its ID, for up to 7 days, so that we do not process the same notification twice.
  • If someone replies STOP, we keep their Instagram-scoped ID so that none of your automations message them again.

2.2.4. How we use it

  • To build your portfolio and keep its numbers current. A published portfolio is refreshed at least every three days.
  • To show you your portfolio in the editor and let you choose what it shows
  • To run the automations you create: replying under matching comments, sending the messages you wrote, and reporting how many people received and opened your link
  • To keep your Instagram connection working, including renewing your access token before it expires

We do not use your Instagram data for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models or send it to AI services.

2.2.5. Your public portfolio

When your portfolio is published, anyone with its link can see the sections you have left on, and download it as a PDF. You can switch off any section, which removes it from the page entirely, add a password, or unpublish the portfolio at any time from the editor.

2.2.6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent Scoop Kit data. We use service providers for hosting and network delivery, who process data only to provide those services to us. Section 2.1.4 also applies to Scoop Kit.

2.2.7. Deleting your data

You can delete everything Scoop Kit holds about you in either of these ways:

  • In Instagram, go to Settings, then Apps and websites, remove Scoop Kit, and choose to delete your data. Instagram sends us the request and we delete your data immediately. The confirmation links to a page where you can check the request.
  • Sign in at https://kit.scoop.app/data-deletion and choose to delete your data.

Deleting removes your profile, insights, stored pictures, portfolio and automations, and your portfolio link stops working.

If you remove Scoop Kit in Instagram without asking for deletion, we stop accessing your account, stop your automations and unpublish your portfolio straight away, so its link stops working. We keep your data for 90 days in case you connect again, then delete it. You can also email legal@scoop.app.
