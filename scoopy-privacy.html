This policy explains how Scoopy, the iPhone app made by Scoop, collects, uses, stores and shares your information. It applies alongside the Scoop Privacy Policy, which covers all Scoop products; where the two differ, this page applies to Scoopy. Scoopy's Terms of Service are in section 15 of the Scoop Terms.

1. What Scoopy is

Scoopy is an AI assistant for creators. It connects to your Instagram account, and optionally your YouTube channel, to help you find and manage brand partnerships: it finds brand inquiries in your messages, keeps your deals in one place, shows your audience and content numbers in plain language, builds a media kit you can choose to publish, and drafts replies for you to review. Scoopy only sends a message on your behalf when you approve it. You must be 18 or older to use Scoopy.

2. Information we collect

2.1. Information you give us

  • Your phone number, which we use to create your account and sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your messages to Scoopy, your rates, and the details of your brand deals (brand, deliverables, amounts, dates and payment status).
  • Photos and videos you choose to attach in a chat.
  • An email address, only if you ask for email summaries.

2.2. Information from Instagram

When you connect Instagram through Instagram's own login, we receive the information you grant: your profile (username, name, account type, picture, bio and follower counts), your posts and their insights, your account insights including audience age, gender and location, and, if you allow it, your direct messages and comments. We store an access token, encrypted, to read this information and to send the replies you approve.

2.3. Information from YouTube

If you connect YouTube, you sign in with Google and grant read-only access. Through YouTube API Services we receive:

  • your channel's name, picture and statistics;
  • your videos' titles, thumbnails, publish dates and public counts;
  • your YouTube Analytics, such as views, watch time, subscribers gained, impressions, click-through rate and audience age, gender and country.

We store an access token, encrypted, to refresh this information. Scoopy cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on anything on your channel.

2.4. Information from your device

A push notification token, if you allow notifications, and anonymous usage events (for example which screens are opened) linked to a random internal identifier, never to your messages, names or amounts.

3. How we use it

We use your information only to provide and improve Scoopy's features for you:

  • to find brand inquiries in your messages and keep track of your deals;
  • to show your Instagram and YouTube numbers, trends and milestones, and to suggest rates based on them;
  • to build a media kit that you choose whether to publish;
  • to draft replies and pitches that you review and approve before anything is sent;
  • to keep your account secure and to notify you when something needs your attention.

AI. Scoopy uses large language models to read your messages and data, answer your questions and write drafts. Your content is processed by AI model providers only to provide this service. Your information, including Google user data, is not used to train or improve AI models, ours or anyone else's. Scoopy does not generate images or video of people, and does not use Google user data for any purpose other than the features described here.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. How we share it

We share your information only with service providers that process it on our behalf to run Scoopy, each bound to use it only for that purpose:

  • AI model providers, to analyse your content and generate drafts and suggestions;
  • cloud hosting and database providers in the United States, to store your data;
  • Meta, to read your connected Instagram account, send the replies you approve, and deliver your sign-in code by WhatsApp;
  • Google, to read your connected YouTube channel;
  • an email provider, for emails you ask for; Apple, for push notifications; and a product analytics provider, for the anonymous usage events described above.

We may also disclose information when required by law, or as part of a merger or acquisition, with notice to you. Google user data is transferred to others only as needed to provide the features you see in Scoopy, to comply with law, or as part of such a transaction.

5. Storage and retention

Your data is stored on servers in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest, with each creator's data kept apart at the database level. We keep your data for as long as you have a Scoopy account. YouTube data is refreshed regularly while YouTube is connected and deleted when you disconnect. Attached photos and videos are kept until you delete them. Database backups are kept for up to 7 days.

6. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to your profile and choose Delete account. We delete your account and all of its data, including stored Instagram and YouTube data and tokens. You can also email legal@scoop.app.
  • Disconnect YouTube: in the app, or by revoking Scoopy's access in your Google security settings. Either way we delete your stored YouTube data.
  • Disconnect Instagram: in the app, or by removing Scoopy under Apps and websites in Instagram's settings.

7. Google API Services

Scoopy uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy describes how Google handles your data.

Scoopy's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you see in Scoopy; we do not use it for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models; and people at Scoop do not read it except with your permission, for security, or to comply with law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise them, use the controls in the app or contact us at legal@scoop.app. Section 5 of the Scoop Privacy Policy applies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will tell you about material changes in the app and by updating the date at the top.
